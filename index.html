About me

Hi, I’m a professional graphic designer with more than ten years of experience, based in Huddersfield, UK.

I started in UX design and video, moved into freelance work with fashion and lifestyle brands, and today lead visual design at Globital. I combine design, storytelling and an understanding of consumer behaviour to connect brands with their audiences.

I studied computer science (BSCS) at the National College of Business Administration & Economics in Lahore, Pakistan, from 2014 to 2018.
